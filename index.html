Sky Shooter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Sky Shooter game ("the game") handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is developed by Jelbert ("we").

Summary

  • No sign-up, no email, no real name, no contacts.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob; Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure them (see Advertising). In the EU / UK you're asked first; in some US states you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data in Settings.
  • To run the online leaderboard, the game creates an anonymous player account and stores your pilot name, best score, stage reached and date.
  • Your pilot name, best score, stage and date are shown publicly on the in-game leaderboard.
  • Everything else (settings, tutorial progress) stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file on your device with your best score, your coins, ships and upgrades, your settings (sound, music, screen shake, flashing, vibration, drag speed), whether you finished the tutorial, your pilot name, and the sign-in token of your anonymous player account. It never leaves your device except as described below. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Information stored online

When your device is online, the game signs in to our leaderboard service as an anonymous player. We then store:

InformationWhyWho can see it
Anonymous player ID (a random identifier, not linked to your identity)To keep your best score attached to your player between sessionsOnly us
Pilot name (1-12 letters/digits you choose; optional, defaults to a callsign like PILOT-1A2B)To show your entry on the leaderboardEveryone, on the leaderboard
Best score, stage reached, date achievedThe leaderboardEveryone, on the leaderboard

Please don't use your real name or other personal details as your pilot name. Names are filtered for offensive words, and we may rename or remove entries that break this policy.

The leaderboard runs on Supabase (supabase.com), which hosts the database for us as a service provider. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not sell or share this information with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between games, and short ads you can choose to watch for bonus coins after a run. Coins are only earned by playing; nothing in the game is sold for real money.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location it gives,
  • information about the ads shown and how you interact with them, and app diagnostics.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

In US states with their own privacy laws (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Utah), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. After you opt out, Google shows only ads that aren't personalized to you. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects for ads; we don't receive your advertising ID or location.

Retention and deletion

  • Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs.
  • To delete your online data at any time, open Settings → Delete Online Data in the game. This immediately deletes your leaderboard entry and your anonymous player account, and switches the online leaderboard off on your device (your best score stays on the device; you can rejoin later as a new player).
  • You can also email jelbertnixonbasco@gmail.com with your pilot name (and roughly when you set your best score) and we'll delete your entry and account within 30 days.
  • You can switch the online leaderboard off at any time in Settings → Online Leaderboard; while it's off the game makes no leaderboard connections (ads still load).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a pilot name, contact us and we'll remove it.
